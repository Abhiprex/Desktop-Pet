# Puppy & Cat Desktop Pets 🐶🐱

An interactive dual-pet desktop companion featuring a lively **Golden Puppy** and a cozy **Orange Tabby Kitten** sharing your desktop!

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Dual Interactive Pets**:
  - **Puppy 🐶**: Energetic, floppy-eared golden pup with a red collar, golden tag, wagging tail, and playful personality.
  - **Kitten 🐱**: Sleek orange tabby with white socks, emerald eyes, pointy pink-lined ears, and graceful movements.
- **Dedicated Interactive Furniture**:
  - **Puppy Bed 🛏️🐶**: Deep blue bolster pet bed with back pillow and bone emblem.
  - **Kitten Bed 🛏️🐱**: Soft pastel pink circular fleece nest with mint cushion and paw emblem.
  - **Puppy Bowl 🥣🐶**: Royal blue dog bowl with kibbles on the left and fresh water on the right.
  - **Kitten Bowl 🥣🐱**: Pastel lavender saucer with fresh tuna bites on the left and creamy milk on the right.
  - **Toy Ball ⚽**: Bouncy multi-color play ball that both pets love to chase and kick!
- **Interactive Mechanics**:
  - **Gravity & Physics**: Pets and furniture can be picked up and dragged anywhere. When dropped, gravity smoothly pulls them to settle on the floor/taskbar.
  - **Bed & Bowl Interaction**: Double-click any bed to send the pet to take a nap; double-click any bowl to feed them. If you drag their bed or bowl away, they will wake up or run over to it!
  - **Snuggle & Cuddle Mode**: Double-click or multi-click any pet to trigger a joyful cuddle with flying floating hearts (`💖`, `💕`, `✨`)!
  - **Cursor Follow Mode**: Toggle cursor following and watch both pets trot alongside your mouse pointer.
  - **Autonomous Social AI**: When puppy and kitten cross paths, they greet each other and play together!

## 🚀 How to Run

- **Direct Exe**: Double-click `Desktop Pet.exe` (native Win32 GUI executable with embedded icon).
- **Silent Script**: Double-click `Launch Desktop Pet.vbs` or `run_pet.pyw`.
- **Command Line**:
  ```bash
  python main.py
  ```

## 🎮 Controls & Shortcuts

| Action | Target | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Left Click & Drag** | Puppy / Kitten / Furniture | Pick up and move anywhere on screen; settles down with gravity |
| **Double Click** | Puppy or Kitten | Trigger Snuggle Mode + flying hearts (`💖`, `💕`, `✨`) |
| **Double Click** | Puppy Bed or Kitten Bed | Send the corresponding pet to sleep in bed with floating Zzz's |
| **Double Click** | Puppy Bowl or Kitten Bowl | Send the corresponding pet to eat/drink with delicious nibbling |
| **Double Click / Click** | Toy Ball | Kick and bounce the rubber ball across screen |
| **Left Click** | ⚙️ Floating Settings Button | Instantly open full Action & Settings menu (no chasing moving pets!) |
| **Left Drag** | ⚙️ Floating Settings Button | Reposition the settings button anywhere convenient on your screen |
| **Right Click** | Pets, Furniture, or Tray Icon | Open Full Action & Settings Menu |

### 🐾 Mutual Actions (Both Pets Options)
- **💖 Cuddle & Snuggle Both**: Both pets hop joyfully with happy snuggles and a shower of floating hearts!
- **🥣 Call Both to Eat**: Both pets trot directly to their food bowls and enjoy an uninterrupted meal!
- **💤 Bedtime for Both**: Both pets snuggle into their cozy beds and sleep peacefully until woken!
- **☀️ Wake Up Both / Free Roam**: Wakes both pets up from bed or bowls into lively free roaming play!
- **🐾 Follow Mouse Cursor (Toggle)**: Responsive real-time trotting alongside your mouse pointer with vertical jump reactions!
- **⚽ Spawn / Bounce Toy Ball**: Bounces a lively rubber toy ball that both pets chase and kick!
- **📦 Put Away Toy Ball**: Packs up and hides the toy ball so pets return to normal roaming!
